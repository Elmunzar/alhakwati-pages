AlHakwati

Your storyteller — books and papers, read aloud

HomeSupportPrivacy

The hakawati (حكواتي) was the storyteller of the Arab coffee house, bringing tales to life by voice alone. AlHakwati brings that tradition to your phone: give it a book or a paper and it reads the whole thing to you, in the voice you choose.

Read anything

EPUB books, PDFs (including scientific papers), plain or pasted text, and photos or scans of printed pages.

Your choice of voice

Natural built-in voices in many accents and languages, free and offline — or your own voice, recorded in the app.

Made for papers, too

Reads statistics, units and medical terms properly, skips citations like [12] and reference lists, and finds the Abstract, Methods, Results and Discussion for you.

Listen your way

Keeps reading with the screen locked. Change speed, go back 10 minutes, set a sleep timer, save notes — by tap or by asking Siri. Pauses for phone calls and, if you like, when someone starts talking to you.
