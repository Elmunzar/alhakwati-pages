Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

HomeSupportPrivacy

AlHakwati is designed to keep your reading private. Every voice reads on your device. We (the developer) do not run any servers for the app, do not create accounts, and do not collect any data, analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking data. The app does not send your books, notes or voice anywhere.

Your books and notes

Books you import, your reading positions and your notes are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere by us. They are included in your device's own backups (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android) under Apple's or Google's terms. The same applies to files you open or share into AlHakwati from other apps, such as Files, WhatsApp, Mail or Praxia Docs: they are read on your device and are not uploaded.

Built-in voices

When you use a built-in (“generic”) voice — or Apple Personal Voice on iPhone and iPad — speech is generated by your device's own text-to-speech engine. With voices that are fully installed, no text or audio leaves the device. On Android, a few voices are marked as needing an internet connection; if you choose one of those, your device's speech engine (for example Google's) processes the text under its provider's privacy policy.

Microphone

The microphone is used only if you turn on Pause when someone talks to me. Sound is then analysed on the device to notice speech; it is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you scan or choose pictures of book pages. Text is recognised on your device; the pictures are not stored by the app or uploaded.

Siri and Google Assistant

Voice commands such as “Pause AlHakwati” (Siri) or “Hey Google, pause” (Android) are handled by Siri or Google Assistant under Apple's or Google's privacy policy. AlHakwati only receives the command itself.

Feedback

If you choose Send feedback, AlHakwati opens your own email app with a message addressed to ebappsdevelopment@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app, and you can read and change the message first. If you leave Include phone details on, the message also contains the app version, your phone's system version and model, the app language, the type of voice you are using and your reading speed. It never contains your books, notes or file names. We use feedback only to improve the app and to reply to you.

Delete your data

AlHakwati keeps your data on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Books, notes and reading places: delete a book in the app (Delete book), or uninstall AlHakwati to remove everything it stored on your device. Copies in your device backups follow your Apple or Google backup settings.
  • Feedback emails: write to ebappsdevelopment@gmail.com and we will delete the emails you sent us within 30 days.

We (the developer) hold no other data about you.

Children

AlHakwati is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: ebappsdevelopment@gmail.com.
