Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

HomeSupportPrivacy

AlHakwati is designed to keep your reading private. We (the developer) do not run any servers for the app, do not create accounts, and do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking data.

Your books and notes

Books you import, your reading positions and your notes are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere by us. They are included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup) under Apple's terms.

Built-in voices

When you use a built-in (“generic”) or Apple Personal Voice, all speech is generated on your device. No text or audio leaves the device.

Personalised voices (ElevenLabs)

If you choose to create or use a personalised voice, the app connects to ElevenLabs using an API key that you provide (stored in your device's Keychain):

  • When you create a voice, the voice recording(s) you choose are sent to ElevenLabs to build the voice.
  • When a book is read with a personalised voice, the text being read is sent to ElevenLabs to produce the audio. The audio is stored on your device so it doesn't need to be generated again.

This data is processed by ElevenLabs under their own privacy policy and terms. You must only create a voice of yourself, or of a person who has given you permission. You can delete personalised voices in the app, which also deletes them from your ElevenLabs account.

Microphone

The microphone is used only (1) when you record a voice sample, and (2) if you turn on Pause when someone talks to me. In the second case sound is analysed on the device to notice speech; it is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you scan or choose pictures of book pages. Text is recognised on your device; the pictures are not stored by the app or uploaded.

Siri

Voice commands such as “Pause AlHakwati” are handled by Siri under Apple's privacy policy. AlHakwati only receives the command itself.

Children

AlHakwati is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: ebappsdevelopment@gmail.com.
