Common questions

How do I add a book?

Tap + in the Library. Import an EPUB, PDF or text file from Files, paste text, scan printed pages with the camera, or choose photos of pages.

Can I protect books with DRM?

AlHakwati reads DRM-free files only. Books bought from stores that lock files with DRM can't be opened by any third-party app.

How do I get more natural voices?

On iPhone open Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices (on some iOS versions Read & Speak › Voices), choose a language and download a voice marked Enhanced or Premium. It then appears in AlHakwati under Voice › Generic voice.

How do I use my own voice?

Choose Voice › Record your own voice, read the passage shown for one to two minutes, and create the voice. Personalised voices are produced by ElevenLabs and need an ElevenLabs account and API key, added in Settings. You can only create a voice of yourself, or of someone who has given you permission.

Can I control it hands-free?

Yes, with Siri: “Pause AlHakwati”, “Resume AlHakwati”, “Rewind AlHakwati”, “Speed up AlHakwati”, “Slow down AlHakwati”, “Stop AlHakwati”, “Save a note in AlHakwati”.

Why did it pause by itself?

It pauses for phone calls and other audio interruptions, when headphones are disconnected, when the sleep timer ends, and — only if you've turned it on — when someone starts talking to you. You can change these under Listening.
