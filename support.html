Common questions

How do I add a book?

Tap + in the Library. Import an EPUB, PDF or text file from Files, paste text, scan printed pages with the camera, or choose photos of pages.

Can it open books with DRM?

AlHakwati reads DRM-free files only. Books bought from stores that lock files with DRM can't be opened by any third-party app.

How do I get more natural voices?

On iPhone open Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices (on some iOS versions Read & Speak › Voices), choose a language and download a voice marked Enhanced or Premium. It then appears in AlHakwati under Voice › Generic voice.

On Android open Settings › Accessibility › Text-to-speech output, tap the settings icon next to your speech engine (for example Speech Services by Google), choose Install voice data and download the languages and accents you want. AlHakwati has a button that opens this screen for you.

How do I use my own voice?

On iPhone and iPad, create an Apple Personal Voice (Settings › Accessibility › Personal Voice — it is made on your device, free and private), then choose Voice › Your own voice (Apple Personal Voice) in AlHakwati. Personal Voice is currently available in English. Other books are read by the built-in voice for their language. Everything stays on your device.

Can I control it hands-free?

On Android, while AlHakwati is reading say “Hey Google, pause”, “Hey Google, resume” or “Hey Google, next”, or long-press the app icon for Continue listening, Back 10 minutes and Save a note. On iPhone and iPad, with Siri: “Pause AlHakwati”, “Resume AlHakwati”, “Rewind AlHakwati”, “Speed up AlHakwati”, “Slow down AlHakwati”, “Stop AlHakwati”, “Save a note in AlHakwati”.

Why did it pause by itself?

It pauses for phone calls and other audio interruptions, when headphones are disconnected, when the sleep timer ends, and — only if you've turned it on — when someone starts talking to you. You can change these under Listening.
